## Project: Strategic Market Entry & Circular Economy Transition (Cabamix)
**Files:** `FINAL SUBMISSION_G01_T05.pdf` (Comprehensive Strategy Report) & `Pitch Hackathon_G01_T05 (1).pdf` (Executive Pitch Deck)

A complete strategic expansion plan for **Cabamix**, a French bio- and mineral-based plastic compound manufacturer, detailing an aggressive, compliant market entry into the highly eco-conscious Scandinavian territory (Sweden, Denmark, and Finland).

### Core Business & Expansion Insights
* **Regulatory Compliance Mapping:** Audited complex Scandinavian environmental targets (e.g., Finland’s 2035 carbon-neutrality target and Sweden’s 2030 recycled packaging mandates) to identify immediate commercial market entry points for advanced green polymers.
* **Circular Business Model Innovation:** Structured a phased 4-stage operational blueprint (Audit ➔ Installation ➔ Label Expansion ➔ Optimization) to transition Cabamix from a traditional linear compound manufacturer into a high-margin circular economy provider utilizing specialized take-back and end-of-life recycling loops.
* **Targeted B2B Strategic Alliances:** Engineered custom product-market fit solutions and R&D partnership proposals for leading regional enterprise clients:
  * **Carlsberg (Denmark):** Sustainable packaging films, wraps, and multi-pack carriers.
  * **Paulig (Finland):** High-temperature resistant, home-compostable coffee capsules (EN13432 certified).
  * **Orkla (Norway/Nordics):** High-performance mineral-filled recycled engineering plastics for technical parts.
  * **Essity (Sweden):** Advanced bio-based barrier films for high-volume consumer product components.
* **Differentiated Competitive Advantage:** Formulated a specialization matrix focusing resource allocation entirely on high value-added, regulatory-proof bio-compounds to rapid-scale brand equity through cross-border corporate alliances.
